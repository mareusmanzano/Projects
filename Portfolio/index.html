I'm a full-stack developer with a soft spot for good typography and well-named variables. I care about the details most people never notice — the 200ms transition, the empty state that actually helps, the error message that doesn't just say "something went wrong."

        
Outside of code, you'll usually find me sketching UI ideas I'll never build, chasing down a good cup of coffee, or losing to my cat at doing literally anything productive.
